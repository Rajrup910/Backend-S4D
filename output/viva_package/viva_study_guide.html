Executive Understanding

Reported accuracy on dermoscopy benchmarks has largely saturated. However, the properties that determine clinical deployability—reliable calibration, knowing when to abstain, and ensuring guarantees hold for vulnerable subgroups—are rarely audited.

One-Sentence Thesis

"Aggregate discrimination benchmarks conceal severe, confidently-wrong subgroup triage failures driven by demographic training priors, which post-hoc calibration, abstention, and marginal conformal guarantees fail to protect, but which can be partially mitigated by group-conditional decision rules priced in clinical biopsy burden."

The Under-40 Melanoma Blind Spot & The Age Rule

In patients under 40, escalation sensitivity collapses to 0.143 (3 of 21 caught) due to training prior skew (4.9% vs 35.5%). Abstention fails because errors are confidently wrong (deferring only 11% of misses).

The Age-Conditional Escalation Rule

y_hat(x) = argmax_c [ p_c(x) + λ_b(x) · 1[c ∈ {AKIEC, BCC, MEL}] ]
